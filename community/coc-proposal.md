**Title:** Code of Conduct: add "No recruiting", and cover the Practice Room

**What changes in CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.**

1. A third rule: "**No recruiting.** Don't recruit for any employer, including your own, in any of the practice's spaces. That covers job posts, referrals, interview prep, and approaching members or their staff about roles. Being between roles is welcome. Give your sector, not your availability."
2. Under "Where it applies", replace "the LinkedIn group" with "the Practice Room, the community's Slack, including direct messages that start there".
3. Under "Reporting", send reports about any editor or co-organizer to the independent conduct reviewer named in EDITORS.md.

**Why.** The code bans pitching yourself for hire, but not recruiting others. A room of planners from competing employers needs that rule written down. Without it, members between roles can't join without being approached, and teams can't share problems without fear of poaching.

**Open period.** 60 days, because this changes governance and there is one editor today. It can close at 30 days once two editors are confirmed. Interim room rules apply in the room from the day it opens. I will close it after the open period, and write down why.
