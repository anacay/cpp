## What changed
- The entity sentence and the definition say "infrastructure capacity planning"; About's "At a glance" gains a Scope row.
- `/what-is-capacity-planning/`: H1 "What is infrastructure capacity planning?"; a first question, "Is this the same as capacity planning for teams and sprints?", answered "No"; FAQ data to match.
- `/questions/`: the same question first; title and lede name infrastructure.
- Titles on the home page, body of knowledge, glossary, term pages, The Commons and Join in carry "infrastructure capacity planning".
- llms.txt gains a "Scope" section.
- README: the first paragraph says infrastructure, not team or project capacity; links the questions page.

- Three housekeeping pages (`/privacy/`, `/changes/`, `/circle/`) carry noindex and leave the sitemap (96 URLs), so search engines judge the site on its substance.
