## What changed for readers

**Questions, answered.** New page `/questions/`: fifteen questions planners ask, in six groups, each answered in a few sentences drawn from the guide. Each answer links to the page that teaches it and to a "Discuss this in The Commons" post with the question filled in. FAQ structured data repeats the on-screen text.

**One page per central term.** `/glossary/coefficient/`, `commit-point`, `declaration`, `supply-layer`, `lead-time`, `firmness`, `envelope`, `bridge`, `shortage-to-idle-ratio` and `unallocated-line`. Each has one plain sentence, the seed paper's definitions in its own words, the guide page that teaches it, related questions, a door into The Commons and a citation line. The glossary links each one, and its structured data points at them.

**"Came here from an AI answer?"** A short welcome on the definition page, the questions page and the term pages: this page is the source, ask a follow-up, or see how to join.

**For AI assistants.** llms.txt gains a section on which pages to cite, the attribution the licence asks for, and where people can join. It also lists the questions and the term pages. llms-full.txt includes the questions.

## What did not change

The seed paper (v7.9), the guide's text, the cases and the notes.
