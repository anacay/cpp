# Contributors

Everyone who has helped build the body of knowledge, by edition, with what they did. Being listed doesn't mean you endorse every word. It means you helped build it.

Credit lands here when your contribution is merged or used, not only at the next edition.

## How credit works

- **When.** You're added once your fix, term, source, case or comment is merged or used. An editor adds you in the same change.
- **How you're named.** Your name, a handle, or anonymous. You can add a sector, such as "cloud" or "supply". Your employer is never required.
- **On the page itself.** A page that changes because of a thread says so at its foot, in a "Changed because of" line. It names the thread and, with your agreement, you.
- **Bylines.** Cases and notes name their author and reviewers at the top of the page.
- **Roles.** People who step down from a [role](https://cpp.anacay.org/roles/) stay listed here as emeritus.

Want a different name, a handle, or to be listed as anonymous? Say so in your pull request or thread, or write to [cpp@anacay.org](mailto:cpp@anacay.org). See [How it grows](HOW_IT_GROWS.md#credit).

Every change, with its date, is on [What changed](https://cpp.anacay.org/changes/).
