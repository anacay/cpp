# Cadence agendas

Five meeting agendas, one per planning cadence: weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual and post-launch. From the Capacity Planning Practice, cpp.anacay.org. CC BY-SA 4.0.

**How to use them.** Copy one into your meeting invite and cut what you don't need. The steps come from the guide's stage pages. The time boxes are illustrative, a starting point to adjust. Each agenda ends with what the meeting hands on, and who owns it.

**One rule for every meeting.** Nobody sees their number for the first time in the room. Walk the numbers up beforehand (guide, People: "No surprises in the room").

---

## Weekly: steer, bridge, test before launch

Guide: https://cpp.anacay.org/guide/weekly/ · 30 minutes (illustrative)

**Comes in.** From monthly: a ledger with named gaps, owned bridges and a ranked list.

| # | Item | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bridges.** Which approved demand can't be sourced in time? Bridge it, fastest lever first. Charge each premium to its cause. | 8 min |
| 2 | **Trades.** Who holds capacity they don't need, and who needs that shape, there, now? Log every trade. | 5 min |
| 3 | **Shortages.** Apply the shortage rules agreed beforehand. Don't split a shortage pro rata. | 5 min |
| 4 | **Turn-up.** What is delivered but not yet live? How old is the queue? | 4 min |
| 5 | **Before launch.** Does any pre-launch test disagree with the declared coefficient? Raise it now. | 4 min |
| 6 | **Escalations and overrides.** Log each one, with what moved down. Every "no" comes with a path forward. | 4 min |

**Goes out, to post-launch.** Live launches and their measured coefficients, bridge premiums and their causes, recorded slips, and replenishments still owed.

| Artifact | Owner |
|---|---|
| Fulfillment ledger: bridges, replenishments owed | Planning carries the gap; each performer owns its entry |
| Forum decision log: overrides, brokered trades, escalations | Named executive forum, run by planning |
| Commitment register: re-pegged supply | Supply chain, with planning and finance |
| Declaration register: slips recorded | Declaring team; planning keeps it |

## Monthly: answer every ask, keep the ledger

Guide: https://cpp.anacay.org/guide/monthly/ · 60 minutes (illustrative)

**Comes in.** From quarterly: dated declarations, medium-lead orders and supply's review.

| # | Item | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Supply's answer.** Every approved ask gets a dated commit or a named gap. Until supply answers, the approval is uncommitted. | 15 min |
| 2 | **The fulfillment ledger.** Every gap named, with an owner and a date. | 10 min |
| 3 | **Approval and allocation.** Treat them as two acts. Approved is not allocated. | 10 min |
| 4 | **Re-shape before you cut.** When memory or IOPS binds, which part of the design really needs it? | 10 min |
| 5 | **Promotions.** Move a declaration up a stage on evidence only. | 5 min |
| 6 | **Bridges ahead.** Name the bridge before you need it, with its owner. | 10 min |

**Goes out, to weekly.** A ledger with every gap named, bridges owned and dated, and a ranked list supply has answered.

| Artifact | Owner |
|---|---|
| Fulfillment ledger | Planning carries the gap; each performer owns its entry |
| Supply plan of record and commitment register: commits and named gaps | Supply chain, with planning and finance |
| Declaration register: Configured, expiry checked | Declaring team; planning keeps it |
| Commit-point map: stocked pool, turn-up | Supply chain, with planning |
| Forum decision log: re-rank, cut-line moves | Named executive forum, run by planning |

## Quarterly: the intake and the supply review

Guide: https://cpp.anacay.org/guide/quarterly/ · 90 minutes (illustrative), after one-to-one talks with each team

**Comes in.** From annual: the envelope, the cut line, intents and signed ratios. From post-launch: actuals and restated coefficients, through the intake.

| # | Item | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Demand in driver units and shapes.** Each ask beside the team's prior ask and prior actual. | 15 min |
| 2 | **Re-forecast against actuals.** Where did last quarter land? | 10 min |
| 3 | **The supply review.** What can supply really do, by when? Name the gaps. | 20 min |
| 4 | **Firm declarations to Dated.** Which intents now have dates? | 10 min |
| 5 | **Agree the coefficients,** and use the right price for each decision. | 10 min |
| 6 | **The high scenario.** Hold it as options or a reserve, not as orders. | 10 min |
| 7 | **Gates.** Where an order is due before the event that would size it: ask earlier, buy an option, or commit to the record. Write down which. | 15 min |

**Goes out, to monthly.** Dated declarations, a supply answer per line, placed orders and agreed coefficients.

| Artifact | Owner |
|---|---|
| Intake: declared demand, prior ask beside prior actual | Consuming team's accountable lead; planning pre-fills |
| Coefficient register and rate card | Service owner, with finance |
| Declaration register: Intent to Dated | Declaring team; planning keeps it |
| Supply plan of record and commitment register | Supply chain, with planning and finance |
| Commit-point map: machines and parts | Supply chain, with planning |
| Forum decision log: refreshed ranked list | Named executive forum, run by planning |

## Annual: the envelope and the slow layers

Guide: https://cpp.anacay.org/guide/annual/ · a series of sessions, closing before finance's budget lock

**Comes in.** The year's post-launch reviews, through the quarterly intakes they fed: restated coefficients, stage records, and asks beside actuals.

| # | Item | Session |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The envelope and the first cut line.** | Leaders and finance |
| 2 | **The re-cut order,** agreed now, so a mid-year cut follows it. | Forum |
| 3 | **Pre-position the longest-lead supply,** mostly as options or cancelable commitments. | Supply and planning |
| 4 | **Slow attributes.** Ask for region, redundancy tier, hardware class and data stored at intent, as ranges. | Teams, at intake |
| 5 | **Strategic bets,** sized from the record. | Forum |
| 6 | **Signatures.** Forecasts, efficiency commitments and shortage-to-idle ratios. | Each owner |
| 7 | **Close before finance's budget lock.** | Planning program lead |

Walk the numbers up first: baselines to leaders, demand to teams and back, then executives, then finance.

**Goes out, to quarterly.** An envelope, a cut line, a re-cut order, intents with ranges and signed ratios.

| Artifact | Owner |
|---|---|
| Annual capacity agreement | Consuming team's leader, with finance |
| Landing rule | Planning, with finance |
| Planning calendar | Planning program lead |
| Forum decision log: ranked list, cut line, re-cut order | Named executive forum, run by planning |
| Declaration register: intents | Declaring team; planning keeps it |
| Commit-point map: slow layers | Supply chain, with planning |

## Post-launch: close the loop

Guide: https://cpp.anacay.org/guide/post-launch/ · 45 minutes (illustrative), a few weeks after a launch is live

**Comes in.** From weekly: live launches, bridge premiums, slips and owed replenishments.

| # | Item | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Actuals become the next coefficient.** Log any discontinuity: a hardware change, a migration, a re-grain. | 8 min |
| 2 | **Split the miss by owner:** volume, coefficient, rate. See `split-the-miss-illustrative.csv`. | 10 min |
| 3 | **The measuring loop.** Route the ratio alert to the service owner, and the forecast alert to the product team. | 5 min |
| 4 | **Close the declaration,** and score it by stage. | 7 min |
| 5 | **Efficiency.** Land it by the agreed rule. | 5 min |
| 6 | **Reclaim** stranded and expired grants. | 5 min |
| 7 | **Re-baseline,** if the plan has stopped being useful. | 5 min |

**Goes out, to the next quarterly intake.** Restated coefficients, new drivers, stage records, each team's ask beside its actual, and reclaims due back.

| Artifact | Owner |
|---|---|
| Coefficient register and rate card: restated versions, new drivers | Service owner, with finance |
| Unused and unallocated lines | Service owner; finance keeps score |
| Discontinuity register | Coefficient owner |
| Landing rule, applied | Planning, with finance |
| Declaration register: scored, closed to Organic | Declaring team; planning keeps it |
| Intake: prior ask beside prior actual | Consuming team; planning supplies data |
| Commitment register: promises scored | Supply chain, with planning and finance |
