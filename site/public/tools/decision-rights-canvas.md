# Decision-rights canvas

One page to write down who decides what in your planning process, before the first argument. From the Capacity Planning Practice, cpp.anacay.org. CC BY-SA 4.0.

**Why.** Every capacity plan is a handshake among capital, demand and supply. The planner sits in the middle, usually with no capacity and no budget. Most fights in the room are about a decision nobody owned. Fill this in once, get each owner to sign it, and use it as the forum's reference. (Guide, People: https://cpp.anacay.org/guide/people/)

**How to use it.**
1. Fill the blank canvas (part 1) for your own organization. Use role names, never people's names, so it survives a reorganization.
2. Compare it with the guide's reading (part 2). Every difference is worth a sentence of why.
3. Take it to the forum. A row nobody will sign is your first agenda item.

**Key.** **D** decides. **S** signs or agrees beforehand. **C** is consulted. **I** is informed. **L** says where the decision is logged.

## Part 2. The guide's reading

This is how the practice's guide places each decision. It is argued, not measured. Your organization may differ for good reasons.

| Decision | Who, in the guide | Logged in | Guide page |
|---|---|---|---|
| The envelope and the first cut line | Capital sets the envelope. The forum ranks against it. | Forum decision log | Annual |
| The re-cut order | Agreed by the forum at the annual cycle, before any cut. | Forum decision log | Annual |
| Volume declared | The product team owns volume. | Declaration register | The coefficient |
| Promoting a declaration | On evidence only: a design review and a place on the launch calendar. | Declaration register | Monthly |
| The coefficient | The service owns it. | Coefficient register | The coefficient |
| The rate | The service owns it, with finance. | Versioned rate card | The coefficient; Three prices |
| Approving an ask | Capital's handshake with demand. | Forum decision log | Approval and allocation |
| Allocating | Supply's handshake. Approved is not allocated until supply answers. | Supply plan of record | Approval and allocation; Monthly |
| Shortage rules | The forum agrees them in advance; supply carries them out. | Forum decision log | Weekly |
| An override | Allowed, but logged with what moved down. Its bridges are charged to it. | Forum decision log | Weekly |
| A bridge premium | Charged to its cause, such as a late ask or a forecast miss. | Fulfillment ledger | Weekly |
| Who pays a coefficient term | A rule agreed with finance before any miss. Without one, the term reaches the caller's bill. | Forum decision log | Seed paper, Section 7 |
| Shortage-to-idle ratios | Signed at the annual cycle, with forecasts and efficiency commitments. | The annual sign-off | Annual; Buffers and reserves |
| The landing rule | Planning, with finance, agreed before any recovery. | Landing rule | Post-launch |
| Reclaiming a grant | A configured launch's grant lapses if its date passes unconfirmed. Log each reclaim. | Forum decision log | Post-launch |
