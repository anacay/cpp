# Sharing safely

How to share what you know about capacity planning without sharing anything that isn't yours to share. It applies on this site, on GitHub and in the Practice Room.

## The short version

- Share the shape of a problem, never its real figures.
- Never name a customer, a vendor contract, a site or an internal system that isn't already public.
- Label every invented number as illustrative.
- Not sure? Write to [cpp@anacay.org](mailto:cpp@anacay.org) first. A person reads it before anything is posted.

The [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [How it grows](HOW_IT_GROWS.md) say what can't be contributed.
