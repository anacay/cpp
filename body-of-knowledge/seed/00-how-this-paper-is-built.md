# How this paper is built

Each layer stands on its own and goes one level deeper. The abstract is for the expert, and the executive summary for a busy leader in finance, engineering or supply chain. Then come a one-page version to pin up and the deep dive (Sections 1–14) for planners. Appendix A holds a worksheet with a full walk-through; Appendix B, the public record on supply behind Section 6. Each appendix stands on its own and can be printed or handed over separately.

The two claims are the part meant to last. The method, the thirteen mechanisms, the stages and every illustrative number are expected to change as people use them.
