# Terms used in this paper

Terms defined where they first appear are not repeated here.

- **Fulfillment**: carrying approved demand through allocation, delivery and turn-up to use (Figure 15, steps 3–6).
- **Apportionment**: spreading cost; where a cited source means this, it's written *cost allocation*.
- **Commit, commit point**: a supply order or promise, and the last date it can be placed; not the capacity reservation, temporary or static, that the SRE chapter calls a *commitment* [7].
- **Unallocated line**: unattributable cost, apportioned by declared policy (the cost-tool sense, not the physical one). Like the unused line, it is owned (mechanism 4).
- **Cut line**: divides a ranked list of asks into funded and unfunded.
- **Forum**: the standing executive body that owns the ranked list and the cut line (mechanism 10).
