## Conduct reports, for now

Right now the practice is one person. The founder is the only editor, and he also handles conduct reports. That's a conflict when a report is about him, so it's written down here.

Until someone unaffiliated with Anacay agrees to review conduct, a report about the founder can go to GitHub instead: use **Report content** on the post or comment, and GitHub applies its own Community Guidelines. You can also write to cpp@anacay.org and say the report is about the founder.

An independent conduct reviewer is the first role the practice will ask a newcomer to fill. They'll be named here.

Emeritus editors are listed here once there are any, and are still credited.
